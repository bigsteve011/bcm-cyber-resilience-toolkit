# Annual Exercise Programme

| Quarter | Exercise | Type | Participants | Objectives | Standards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Operation Frozen Ledger (ransomware) | Tabletop | CMT | Decisions, DORA/GDPR reporting, manual workarounds | DORA Art. 11, ISO 22301 8.5 |
| Q2 | Payments failover to secondary region | Technical DR test | IT, Payments Ops | Prove RTO 4h / RPO 15 min | DORA Art. 11–12 |
| Q2 | Critical cloud provider exit simulation | Walkthrough | Procurement, CIO, Legal | Validate exit plan for HyperCloud EU | DORA Art. 28(8) |
| Q3 | Full-scale immutable backup restore | Technical | Infrastructure | Restore throughput at scale | ISO 27001 A.8.13 |
| Q3 | Pandemic / loss of key staff | Tabletop | Business owners | People dependency, succession | ISO 22301 8.4 |
| Q4 | Insider data exfiltration | Tabletop | CMT, HR, Legal, DPO | Investigation, GDPR notification, HR process | GDPR Art. 33–34 |
| Q4 | Call tree & mass notification test | Live | All staff | Reach ≥ 95% within 60 min | ISO 22301 8.4.3 |

Every exercise produces an after-action report. Actions are tracked in the enterprise risk register with SLAs and reported quarterly to the Risk Committee.
